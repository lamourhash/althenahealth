WHO IS IT FOR

You in the Diaspora

Kenyans in the Diaspora — who need a trusted qualified professional to be their eyes, ears and voice in Kenya.

Your Family in Kenya

Elders, parents or relatives managing chronic illness, hospital stays, post-surgery — who need a trained clinical advocate physically present with them.

OUR SERVICES

Medical Advocacy
  • Presence at consultation, reviewing diagnoses, challenging plans — your family’s clinical voice in the room.
  • Regular clinical updates to you — in plain language across any time zone, via WhatsApp or video call.
Post-Surgery Navigation
  • Discharge planning, wound care oversight, and follow-up — bridging hospital to home safely.
Wellness Check-in
  • "Peace of mind" 1-2 home visits and wellness check-in. Patient directed visits. Identifying and sourcing medical and pharmaceuticals supplies as needed.
Geriatrics & Chronic Illness
  • Diabetes, hypertension — coordinated ongoing clinical management reviewed through a US-trained lens.
Mobile Pharmacy & Lab
  • Monthly pharmaceuticals supply and delivery. Lab-Phlebotomy services coming soon.
